DRUG & LOAD INTERLOCK: avoid routine NSAIDs during the loading phase where possible (mechanistic concern, evidence limited — prescribing rests with the physician). No ballistic loading for 48 h after ESWT.
FLUOROQUINOLONE (current or within 6 months) or recent tendon steroid injection → NO ESWT and no heavy tendon loading.

Quadriceps NMES set-up: electrode 1 over the VMO motor point (distal medial thigh) · electrode 2 over proximal lateral thigh (VL / RF) · knee 60° with strap resistance · intensity is the dose: aim for the strongest tolerable contraction. · TENS: intensity is the key parameter; identical daily settings produce tolerance within days — vary frequency.
Guideline limits: Knee OA — NICE 2022 does not recommend TENS, IFC, ultrasound, laser, pulsed short-wave or NMES (exercise, education & weight management first) H · Patellofemoral pain — electrophysical agents not recommended (2016 consensus) M · NMES after TKA / ACLR is a different indication and is recommended.
CI: pacemaker / ICD · over DVT · pregnancy (abdomen, low back) · active malignancy in field · broken skin · impaired sensation · epilepsy (head / neck) · transthoracic placement.
