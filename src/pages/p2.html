Bias-free decision architecture: screen every compartment before committing to a hypothesis. Tick all suspected conditions, then rank the top 3 in the Working Hypothesis box. Mark L/R where relevant. Hip and lumbar spine are screened on every knee patient (bottom card).
1 · Anterior Knee7 TARGETS
Patellofemoral pain (PFP) — retro/peripatellar pain on squat, stairs, prolonged sitting; diagnosis of exclusion
Patellar tendinopathy — inferior pole, load-dependent (jump/energy storage) • Quadriceps tendinopathy — superior pole
Patellar instability — subluxation / dislocation history; (+) moving apprehension test; J-sign; Beighton
Infrapatellar fat pad (Hoffa) — pain in terminal extension / hyperextension, either side of patellar tendon
Osgood-Schlatter (tibial tubercle) • Sinding-Larsen-Johansson (inferior pole) — adolescent apophysitis
Prepatellar / infrapatellar bursitis — superficial fluctuant swelling, kneeling; warm + fever → septic (p1)
Medial plica — painful click, tender band medial to patella
2 · Medial Knee5 TARGETS
MCL sprain — valgus stress 30° (isolated) / 0° (laxity = combined injury): Gr IGr IIGr III
Medial meniscus tear — joint line tenderness, McMurray, Thessaly → composite p4
Pes anserine bursitis / tendinopathy — 2–5 cm below medial joint line, often with OA
Medial tibiofemoral OA — age ≥ 45, activity-related pain, AM stiffness ≤ 30 min
Saphenous nerve — burning medial knee / leg; Tinel at adductor canal; (+) saphenous neurodynamic test
3 · Lateral Knee5 TARGETS
ITB syndrome — runners / cyclists, pain ~30° flexion at lateral epicondyle; (+) Noble compression, Ober
LCL / posterolateral corner — varus stress 0° / 30°; dial test > 10° side-to-side
Lateral meniscus tear — lateral joint line tenderness, Thessaly, McMurray
Popliteus tendinopathy (downhill running) • Proximal tibiofibular joint instability
Common peroneal nerve — Tinel at fibular neck, dorsum-foot numbness, foot drop
4 · Posterior Knee5 TARGETS
Baker’s (popliteal) cyst — posteromedial swelling, often with OA / meniscal tear; rupture mimics DVT → duplex US
PCL injury — dashboard / fall on flexed knee; (+) posterior sag, posterior drawer
Distal hamstring / biceps femoris tendinopathy — pain on resisted flexion, insertion tenderness
Medial gastrocnemius strain (“tennis leg”) — sudden calf pain on push-off
Popliteal artery aneurysm / entrapment, DVT → vascular red flags p1
5 · Intra-Articular & Global6 TARGETS
ACL rupture — pivot mechanism, “pop”, haemarthrosis < 2 h, giving way → cluster p4
Meniscal tear — catching / locking, delayed effusion (6–24 h) → composite p4
Osteochondral lesion / OCD — adolescent, vague pain, effusion, catching; Wilson test L
Patellofemoral / tibiofemoral OA — crepitus, bony enlargement, no warmth (ACR p4)
Inflammatory / crystal arthritis — warm effusion, AM stiffness > 30 min → p1
Post-op arthrofibrosis — persistent extension loss, ↓ patellar mobility, patella infera
